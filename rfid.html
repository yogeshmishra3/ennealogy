RFID Integration

Ennea Information Systems has studied the depth of RFID technology and its applicability. Ennea Information Systems has built considerable knowledge and experience in a wide variety of RFID applications. With this background, we are intended to implement the RFID solutions to every suitable area for customer satisfaction. A successful RFID implementation requires careful evaluation and selection of appropriate RFID frequencies, tags, readers, antennas, and software. Other essential variables which need to be analyzed and taken into account are, the physics of RFID (for instance, the physical characteristics of radio waves, materials, and the surface consistency of those materials), is absolutely critical for system success, not to mention optimal system performance. The best-designed, most robust RFID devices cannot perform optimally within a particular environment unless they have been configured, tuned and installed particularly for that environment and its related variables. With engaging experienced RFID related professional services, the successful performance of any RFID technology implementation can be achieved.

Ennea Information Systems has committed and considers, solving RFID problems and optimizing RFID deployments for its customers to be its mission.Ennea Information Systems provides consulting and system solutions for wide verity of assets management applications. Our professional team includes Software System Architects as well as experienced Hardware Engineers.

Ennea Information Systems is involved right from identifying and quantifying opportunities for improvement and researching for optimum solutions, solution design, development deployment followed by continuous post production support. Our simple approach is to identify customer's business case, prove out our concept and implement a sustainable RFID solution for better ROI(Return Of Investment). Because we also produce our own tags, we help you select the appropriate RF tag and recommend the optimal tag placement, without the mark-ups other companies charge. Our platform includes tags, antennas, readers, middleware, application software, and a wide range of customized RFID products suited for specific system solutions. Our tags and application are suitable for applications ranging from supply chain management to item and asset management, retail operations, manufacturing, health care, law offices, libraries etc.

Ennea Information Systems deals in different tag types and readers in a wide variety of wavelengths in order to accommodate a specialized and customized range of applications. Our tags are available in different forms in order to accommodate all applications–factory programmed, read/write with sophisticated algorithms that avoid collisions and allow very fast and accurate reading. Ennea Information Systems is using a wide range of readers to accommodate the tag and application type. These readers are either board modules or closed box. Our readers can work in a read and read/write mode. We are targeting the scope of RFID technology, by focusing on three primary areas: Returnable Transport Item Management, Inventory/Supply Chain Management and Warehouse Management. We have developed targeted solutions that provide companies with unimpeded visibility into their entire supply chain, regardless of how complex or unique their processes are. We offer professional services to assist you in the areas of assessing needs and requirements, design, planning, consulting, integration, systems implementation, training, asset tagging, and physical asset inventories.
