About us

At Ennea Information Systems, our mission is to become a globally recognized Indian technology company specializing in software and hardware development, M2M solutions, wireless communication, RFID solutions, and electronics devices supply. We are a next-generation technological firm delivering services across diverse sectors such as industrial automation, M2M integration, RFID technology, healthcare management, mining solutions, and automated meter reading.

We take pride in designing custom products and solutions, leveraging our expertise in digital hardware, RF, ASIC/FPGA, software, and embedded systems to deliver cutting-edge engineering solutions. Our dedication is to building successful, market-leading products that reflect our commitment to excellence.

Careers at Ennea Information Systems

Are you seeking a career, not just a job? If so, you've come to the right place! At Ennea Information Systems, we're focused on fostering careers and building a prosperous future for all. In a collaborative environment that encourages innovation, our team members play vital roles in every project, allowing for tremendous growth and the opportunity to acquire new skills.

With access to advanced technology and a state-of-the-art lab for design and development, our multidisciplinary engineering teams can thrive and create revolutionary products. We believe in nurturing talent and providing a unique environment for personal and professional development.

Grow with Us

At Ennea Information Systems, we see our employees as key to our success. We provide an opportunity to grow with the company and reach your full potential. Your talents will be an essential part of creating groundbreaking products, and we offer a vibrant, people-centered environment that supports growth and innovation.

Rewards

We recognize the importance of rewarding our team members. Our competitive remuneration packages are on par with industry standards, with annual appraisals and increments. We also provide comprehensive insurance policies for employees and their families, including life and personal accident coverage. Our employees are our most valuable asset, and we are committed to enhancing their lives.
